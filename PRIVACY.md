# Privacy Policy

**LinkedIn Company Filter** · Last updated: October 6, 2026

LinkedIn Company Filter is a Chrome extension that hides job listings from companies you choose to block while you browse LinkedIn job search.

## Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, or usage information.

## Data stored on your device

The list of company names you choose to block is saved in Chrome's synced storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). It stays in your browser and, if you use Chrome sync, is synced across your own devices by Google. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.

## Website access

The extension runs only on linkedin.com. It reads the company name shown on each job card in order to hide cards from companies on your blocklist. This happens entirely in your browser; no page content leaves it.

## Network requests and remote code

The extension makes no network requests and does not load or run remote code.

## Affiliation

LinkedIn Company Filter is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LinkedIn Corporation.
